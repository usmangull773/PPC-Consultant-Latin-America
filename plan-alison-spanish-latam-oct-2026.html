01

What we found

The Spanish site is fully launched and demand already exists across the region. The pilot's job is to buy Spanish signups in the right countries at a cost you can scale, then show which of those learners go on to pay for a certificate or diploma. Three things shape how it should be built.

High impact

Translation slips on key Spanish pages will hold down ad relevance

Several Spanish URLs and the homepage description read as literal translations. Google scores landing page relevance, so these small fixes lower cost per click before a single bid changes.

The 30 day plan

Measurement first, then a narrow launch, then expansion and automation only once there is conversion data to learn from.

Pilot timeline

1

Track what the Spanish section actually produces

Separate conversion actions for Spanish signups, course starts, and certificate or diploma purchases with their value, all split by country.

Why: without this, bidding optimises toward clicks and the pilot cannot prove its worth.

2

Launch Search in Mexico and Colombia on intent clusters

Ad groups for "cursos gratis con certificado", "curso de excel gratis", "curso de inglés gratis" and the strongest career topics, each landing on its matching Spanish category page, not the homepage.

Why: tight query to page matching lifts relevance and conversion rate on low cost Latin American clicks.

3

Add Microsoft Ads in Mexico, extend Google to three more countries

Import the winning Mexico structure into Microsoft Ads and roll Google out to Argentina, Peru and Chile with their own budgets and cost per signup targets.

Why: country level budgets show where each euro returns the most learners.

4

Hand the strongest cluster to AI bidding

Once conversion volume is there, move to value based bidding toward certificate buyers and test Performance Max against Search.

Why: automation works best on clean data, so it comes after tracking has proven itself.

5

Report and set the scale plan

Day 30 review: cost per signup and certificate revenue by country, keyword and channel, with a recommendation on where to scale.

Why: the pilot ends with a clear decision on long term budget.
